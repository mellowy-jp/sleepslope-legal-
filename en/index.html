Sleep Slope

Don't bet on one alarm. Get several.

An alarm app that spreads several alarms across the stretch between when you'd like to wake and the deadline you cannot miss. This page is where you can get help with Sleep Slope.

Frequently asked questions

My alarm didn't go off

On iOS 26 and later, Sleep Slope rings through the system's own alarm feature (AlarmKit). It rings regardless of the silent switch or Focus modes, but please check the following.

  • That you chose Allow on the alarm permission prompt shown at first launch. If you chose Don't Allow, turn it on under Settings > Apps > Sleep Slope
  • That the alarm card's switch is on. A single alarm switches itself off after it rings
  • With snooze on, the last alarm rings again every 5 minutes until it is stopped. Tapping “I'm up” on the Lock Screen card, or sliding in the app, ends the rest of that morning

On iOS 25 and earlier, alarms ring as notifications, so device settings affect them: check that the silent switch is not set to silent, that any active Focus mode lists Sleep Slope under Settings > Focus > the mode > Allowed Notifications, and that Settings > Notifications > Sleep Slope has notifications allowed and Sounds on. In that case the sound stops on its own after a few dozen seconds.

Either way, on days when being late is not an option, use another method alongside it.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing up

Open “Upgrade” from the Settings tab and tap “Restore Purchases” at the bottom of that screen. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I want to cancel

You can cancel any time in Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your device. App Store subscriptions are managed by Apple, so we cannot cancel one on your behalf.

I'd like a refund

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Please request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I changed phones and my records are gone

Records and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to a server, so there is no way to carry them across devices. Sorry for the inconvenience.
