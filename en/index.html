Sleep Slope

Don't bet on one alarm. Get several.

An alarm app that spreads several alarms across the stretch between when you'd like to wake and the deadline you cannot miss. This page is where you can get help with Sleep Slope.

Frequently asked questions

My alarm didn't go off

Sleep Slope rings through iOS notifications, so your device settings affect it. Check these three things.

  • Whether the silent switch is set to silent
  • Whether a Focus mode (such as Do Not Disturb) is on. If it is, go to Settings > Focus > the mode in question > Allowed Notifications and add Sleep Slope
  • Whether Settings > Notifications > Sleep Slope has notifications allowed and Sounds turned on

Note that the notification sound stops on its own after a few dozen seconds — it is not designed to ring continuously. On days when being late is not an option, use another method alongside it.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing up

Try “Restore purchases” on the Account screen. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I want to cancel

You can cancel any time in Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your device. App Store subscriptions are managed by Apple, so we cannot cancel one on your behalf.

I'd like a refund

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Please request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I changed phones and my records are gone

Records and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to a server, so there is no way to carry them across devices. Sorry for the inconvenience.
